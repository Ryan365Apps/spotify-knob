Radial · screen states · rev W

The value is the label

A percentage means volume. A timestamp means seek. Green and amber say the same thing again. Nothing on the dial screens needs a word on it — so the number gets the centre, and a phyllotaxis bloom, cut by a wedge, does the reading you do at a glance. The idle screen is the same bloom with nothing lit.

Panel ST77916 · 360×360 · 1.8″ round Display face Michroma UI face Montserrat

Rings read outward from one green dot at twelve: the glyph beneath it is lit, and every other glyph fades with angular distance. The arc and the twin ticks are gone — three marks for one selection was two too many. Drawn 1:1 in pixels — the physical screen is ~46 mm across, so this reads about twice life size on a monitor.

09 A — first run, and the recovery path

No network yet

The device cannot serve a page on a network it has not joined, so with no credentials stored it becomes an access point of its own. The QR is a standard WIFI: payload — the iOS and Android camera apps both recognise it and offer “Join network”, which lands you on the captive portal without typing an SSID, a password or a URL.

Encodes
WIFI:T:WPA;S:Radial-A3F2;P:a3f2setup;;
Password
Derived from the MAC, printed under the code — not a secret, and valid only in setup mode
Triggered by
No credentials in NVS, or three failed joins in a row
Why the second
A new router would otherwise brick the device permanently
Fallback
SSID and IP in plain text — a scanner that fails is never the only way in

The AP stays WPA2, not open. The password you type on the portal is your real Wi-Fi password; on an open AP it would cross the air in the clear. That costs four QR modules and is worth it.

14 — the waggle overlay, 2 s

Without leaving Spotify

Flick the dial left-right-left in one wrist movement and this drops over whatever you were looking at. The album art is still underneath, dimmed, because you have not gone anywhere — two seconds later it is gone and the dial is volume again.

Fires on
Three direction reversals inside 600 ms
Safe because
An overshoot correction contains a pause; a waggle does not. The gap is the discriminator, not the count
Volume cost
None. A waggle nets to zero, so the 400 ms write debounce cancels it before anything reaches Spotify
Haptic
A double click — the only one in the product that is not a single

A symmetric gesture cannot carry direction. So this is a plain toggle and the overlay is the only feedback — which is the trade for never leaving the app you were in. When you want certainty rather than speed, 13 A / 13 B are a persistent screen that says the same thing without a timer.

P3 — the same socket, the same five steps

The phone and the dial are reading one stream. Which means you can watch either, look away from both, or hand the phone to someone else — and the device still tells the truth about where it got to.

The Spotify token is a third step, and it is not typed either. tools/spotify_auth.py runs on the PC, opens the browser, takes the approval, and POSTs the token straight to radial.local. Nothing about Spotify is ever copied by hand.

P4 — the editor

Five controls, no more

Drag to reorder, because order is the whole point — a fixed position is what lets your hand learn the list. Pick a glyph from a fixed set. Type a label. Choose a chord from a dropdown. Delete. There is deliberately no icon upload, no free-text command, and no import from the Start menu.

at the cap

What happens at eight

ADD ENTRY greys out and the count turns amber. It does not paginate and it does not scroll, because a ninth entry does not make the launcher better — it makes every position one you have to read rather than know. If eight is genuinely not enough, the escape hatch is groups: a ring of categories, each opening a ring of apps. That is designed but not built, and it should stay unbuilt until eight has actually failed in use.

Still open. Michroma's width is the live question — see 01. If most titles scroll, the alternatives are a condensed face for the title only, or accepting the scroll as part of the device's character. And screen 04 remains the real one: a dial that does nothing on the phone, which is where you listen.
