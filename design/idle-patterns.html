Dial4Spotify · idle screen · six candidates

What it does when
you're not listening

The idle screen is what the device shows for most of the day, so it decides what the object feels like sitting on a desk. Six generative fields, each with one parameter drifting slowly enough that the figure reorganises rather than merely turns. All tinted from the last cover, all with the clock, none with a caption.

Drawn 1:1 at 360 px, animating live. The cost figure is the number of primitives per frame — the practical ceiling on an ESP32-S3 is a few hundred at 2 fps, so treat anything marked dear as needing a pre-rendered buffer rather than a live redraw.

06 — phyllotaxis bloom

Bloom

Sunflower packing. The divergence angle drifts either side of the golden angle by a fraction of a degree, and the spiral arms reorganise completely — the strongest "detail actually changes" of the six.

Drifting
Divergence angle, ±0.4° around 137.5°
Character

Cost
~420 segments · moderate

How to choose. These are all handsome in isolation; the question is which one you want in your peripheral vision for hours. 01 and 05 disappear when you are not looking at them. 03 and 06 keep asking for attention, which is either the point or exactly wrong. 02 is the closest to the reference. 04 is the only one that does not read as a line drawing, so it sits oddly beside the ray fields on the dial screens.

Tint is the placeholder cover's orange throughout. On the device it comes from the average of the last decoded cover, so the whole field shifts with the music.
