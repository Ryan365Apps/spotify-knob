twelve o'clock

Phyllotaxis packs points by golden angle, so consecutive points land 137.5° apart and there is no order around the rim to sweep along. That leaves two honest ways to show a value in it — fill it outwards, or cut a wedge through it. They read completely differently.

Both variants use the same 520-point annular bloom, seeded so the innermost ring sits clear of the numerals. Live at 360 px, values fixed at 62% and 1:47 of 3:52 to match the other mockups.

A — volume, filled outwards

Bloom fills

Points light in index order, which in a phyllotaxis means strictly outwards from the centre. Loudness becomes size — the bloom grows as you turn up. No sweep, no clock face, and nothing arbitrary about where it starts.

The one that behaves correctly

Mixing them is allowed. A for volume and D for seek is defensible — volume genuinely is a quantity and seek genuinely is a position, so letting each take the form that suits it says something true. The cost is that the two dial screens stop being the same widget in two colours, which is what made the earlier revision cohere. Pick one behaviour for both, or accept that they are different instruments.

On the device. 520 short segments is more than the ray field, and unlike the idle screen this redraws while the dial is actually moving. Pre-render the unlit bloom once into a buffer at boot and draw only the lit segments live — that drops the per-frame work to whatever is currently on, which at low volume is almost nothing.
