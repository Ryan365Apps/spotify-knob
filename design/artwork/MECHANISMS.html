Where Everything Fits

Every bought component placed exactly as the v7.1 model puts it, cut open one mechanism at a time. Coloured solids are bought parts; light grey is printed structure; the dark grey faces are where the section knife passed. All dimensions in millimetres, azimuths measured anticlockwise from the +X axis, with the ports at 90°.

02b-carrier

Carrier drive

Vertical section at 350°, through the gearmotor. This is the mechanism that changes the detent strength — it shifts the two magnet rings relative to each other.

1
2
3
4
  1. Gearmotor Ø8 × 20tangential axis at r 36, z 4 — lies on the pad
  2. Worm Ø4.5 × 7drives a toothed tab under ring A at 324°
  3. Carrier ring Az 4.8–6.0, worm-driven, +1.5°
  4. Carrier ring Bz 6.2–7.3, turned −1.5° by the rocker at 255°
Note. Click strength runs 0–100 % in about 50 ms and the worm holds it there without power. The torque needed to turn ring A has not been measured — do it on the printed rings.

  1. 623ZZ bearing3 × 10 × 4 · r 52.63 · z 19–23, inside a Ø13.2 printed V-collar
Note. The collar and eccentric bush are drawn from their print-orientation exports, so their position in this one section is indicative rather than exact. The bearing itself is placed from the model.
