# the 60 — version 14

Built 6 September 2026 (late). v14 is v13 (`docs/v13/`: the encoder reading a code ring under the crown, seat 26.0) with **the design-changes list** (`the60-design-changes.md`, from `GROUNDING.md`, `CONNECTORS.md`, `HALO-BRIGHTNESS.md`, `THERMAL-PLAN.md`, `SYSTEM-REVIEW.md`) built in: Pico-Lock connectors at 2.0 mm on every board; the aluminium core's internal faces bare (no masked pads); the grounding features — one chassis bond, the rim ring's dedicated bond screw, the motor frame's bond lead, the knob's 1 MΩ bleed contact, the USB-C shell wire; the halo current computed from the strip as built (2.54 A); the rim's edge face plain (engine turning removed) with the intake grooves opened to 514 mm²; the vibration actuator's pogo-pin board in a clip; the rotor sensor lead with a service loop. **Height unchanged at 35.9 (knob top 34.4).** 0 check failures in both clutch states across 300 bodies; 13 A3 sheets; 10 printed parts + the three-piece plate.

## What v14 changes against v13, and why

Each item is the design-changes list's, built; the list itself is edited down to what remains.

1. **Connectors** (item 1, `CONNECTORS.md`): every board's connector allowance is 2.0 mm (Pico-Lock 1.50 right-angle, on the edge facing what it connects to); the two USB links are pre-made moulded leads at 4.0. The motion board's envelope goes from 7.4 to 3.6 + a 4.0 plug, the audio's from 6.0 to 3.6 + 4.0. Nothing in the height chain moves — the adapter's socket still sets the seat — but the boards are honest now.
2. **Anodising** (item 2): the core's internal faces are chromate or bare; the closing plate's outer face stays black. The five masked pads are gone from the parameter block, the drawings and the spec. Cost per `GROUNDING.md`: 0.048 W/K.
3. **Grounding** (item 3, `GROUNDING.md`). One chassis bond: an M3 tapped blind hole at (48, 27) on the free side of the port slot with an external-tooth star washer and a ring terminal under the head — the −y side of the slot is taken by the audio board's USB plug and the blower's reserved envelope, so it is 45 mm from the barrel inlet rather than beside it; the production carrier's star point lands here. The rim ring's dedicated bond: an M3 from the top through a Ø3.4 clearance in the ring's flange into a hole tapped 3.0 into the core's shoulder, star washer under the head, both faces bare, at az 56.25 on the land between two intake grooves (the rear arc's lands round the slot are all taken by the exhaust grooves and the ring screws), 35 mm from the chassis bond — hidden under the structure, thread-locked. The motor frame's bond lead: a ring terminal under the motor's inboard base screw, the lead inward over the carriage, up and across to the motion board's ground (modelled, three segments, slack for the 2.4 mm travel). The knob's bleed: a phosphor-bronze leaf (ASSUMED 4 wide, 0.2 thick) screwed to a 5 × 8 × 1 boss on the seat flange at 200°, rising to bear lightly on the crown's underside at r 71.5 — the smallest radius the knob offers (the lens ends at 70, the code ring starts at 72.5), hidden, and wired to the chassis bond through 1 MΩ in the lead; the checker confirms it touches. The USB-C shell wire: four segments from the receptacle's board up out of the slot, under the connect bracket's slab (in its 1.5 mm gap over the plate) to the chassis terminal. The 3.5 mm jack's shell touches nothing metal, and the spec now says the port face must stay an insulator.
4. **Halo current** (item 4): `LED_N` and `HALO_PEAK_A` are computed from the strip's radius (53 LEDs at r 84.8, 48 mA each = 2.54 A, 12.7 W). Section 4.8 and the bench item are corrected; the converter's rating is a bench-measured number, not "10 A".
5. **Rim edge** (item 5): the engine turning is removed (its code and parameters deleted, not kept); the edge face is plain with the two 1.0 chamfers. The vent openings stay parametric (`INTAKE_N/W/H/AZ0/AZ1`, `INTAKE_UNDERCUT_H`, `EXHAUST_AZ`). The intake throat is opened from 252 to 514 mm² (grooves 7 × 3.5, 21 of them) inside the asked 500–700, behind a 1.5 mm undercut (620 mm² mouth); to take 3.5-deep grooves the core's shoulder is 5 tall (the ring's flange 3), leaving 1.5 over each groove. The exhaust grooves are the same section (147 mm²): the exhaust is the restriction now, deliberately kept to the rear arcs (rule 10). The checker warns if the throat leaves the 500–700 band.
6. **The two homeless items** (item 6, `SYSTEM-REVIEW.md`). The vibration actuator's pogo-pin board: a 7 × 8 × 1 board (ASSUMED) in a printed clip on the wall's inside at 252.5° — two ribs from r 76 to the wall with a 0.4 groove each, open-topped, the board dropping in from above — its two pins (ASSUMED Ø1.5, 2.2 compressed) landing on the LRA's flex-tail pads at z 11.75 and 14.35. The rotor sensor lead: out of a new 2.4 × 2.2 notch in the carriage shoe's inboard wall, up through a notch beside the plate's tab slot (t +6, between the tab and the servo frame's wall), a 12 mm service loop over the servo's frame, and down into the motion board's connector — the spec now requires a flex-rated lead for the 2.4 mm travel.

Carried from v13 (`docs/v13/V13.md`): the encoder reading the crown, the seat at 26.0; from v12: the deck-less layout, the Pi on the closing plate's lands with the adapter kit on its standoffs, the connect board on its bracket; from v11: the Ø175.4 knob and the heatsink plate.

## Bench items that decide whether v12 survives

1. **The Pi 5 without its fan, now with a board 1.2 mm over its fins**: log the SoC temperature for an hour of the real workload. The adapter blankets the heatsink; the fins' air leaves sideways under it. If it throttles: 15 mm standoffs (the seat allows the socket 2.3 more), or option B (Pi inverted on a plate boss).
2. **The adapter kit's connect board**: measure it (the model has 30 × 46 × 1.2 from a photograph, corner holes assumed) and confirm the panel's 45-way flex mates with its CN2. The bracket's bosses move to its real holes.
3. **The Pi's USB-C lead**: an up-angled plug no taller than 6.5 with the lead leaving its far end.
4. **The code ring under the crown**: stray daylight through the 0.4 mm rim gap at the lens edge reaches the same cavity the sensor looks into (the bore arrangement had the same exposure through the wall's window); prove it on the bench with the lens fitted, and measure the gap variation as the knob is pushed and pulled on its wheels.
5. The lens, the motor base and bell, the diffuser and strip, the Ø175 knob's inertia — as v11's items 3, 4, 5 and 7. **Halo current: 2.54 A at full white** (53 LEDs at 48 mA); the converter is sized from the measured inlet load, not by this alone.
6. **Grounding tests** (`GROUNDING.md` section 7): continuity from the chassis bond to the rim ring, the core and the star point under 0.1 Ω; the tingle test; the hum test; touch with the PC unplugged. The bleed leaf's drag on the knob: must not be felt.
7. **The rim ring's finish** decides whether its bond spot is masked or the star washer cuts through — open.
8. **The thermal plan's measurements** with the two-circuit duct: the passive baseline and the ducted case with a blower taped into the 308–338 plenum's envelope.
