# the 60 — version 15

Built 7 September 2026. v15 is v14 (`docs/v14/`: the design-changes list built in) with **the v15 brief** (`docs/DESIGN-CHANGES.md` items 1–6, the rewritten `THERMAL-PLAN.md` rules 5/7/8/10/11) and **Ryan's four rulings of 6 September**: the blower is a required, fitted part; the internal structure prints upside down on its flat seat; the display's locating nubs become dowel pins in through-holes; the encoder's platform is a separate, height-selectable shim (and the bleed contact's mount follows the same logic). Built: the plate re-proportioned to 2 web / 5 duct / 1 closing plate with piers under every top screw; fins 5 tall; the stainless rim ring with 92 obround openings through its outer wall into an underside groove, 0.3 polished chamfers; the exhaust rebalanced; a Delta BFB0305HA-C blower standing on the plate over the +y rear plenum with the motion board riding on its saddle, discharging through a hood and a trench cut from the top into the ring's rear exhaust groove; the structure flattened for the inverted print. **Height unchanged at 35.9 (knob top 34.4).** 0 check failures in both clutch states across 322 bodies; 16 A3 sheets; 13 printed parts + the three-piece plate.

## What v15 changes against v14, and why

1. **Plate 2/5/1** (brief item 1, rule 5). The web is 2 mm (z 6–8), the duct 5 (z 1–6), the closing plate 1 in its recess. Thread engagement is the real constraint at 2 mm, so every screw from the top — converter 2, audio 4, servo frame 2, connect bracket 2, speaker cradle 2, the blower's 2, the chassis bond — lands on a Ø7 pier hanging 2.5 into the duct: 4.5 of metal for 3.5 of thread, with 2.5 of duct left under each pier so the channel it stands in runs on (a local 2:1 restriction, 7 mm long). The thickening goes downward only; nothing above the web moves. The nine closing screws (v14: six) sit on full-depth islands; the two in the channel bands, at (0, 45) and (0, −57), have cross-cuts either side so the channels they block are joined to their neighbours. The closing plate seals on a 0.2 die-cut gasket (ASSUMED) in a groove of the recess floor, on the duct's outer wall and the walls round the three through-cuts. **Deflection under knob load, before and after: nil** — the knob rides on the wheels on the structure's wall, the structure stands on the plate at its wall foot and the pillars at r 77.2, all inside the solid rim (r ≥ 75.2); the web carries the boards and the servo only. The plain-disc drum modes are in `checks.txt` for the record.
2. **Fins 5 tall, full length** (item 2, rule 11). Item 2 asked for fins only over the compute module and the converter. Neither sits over a channel: since v12 the Pi is in a window through the web and the duct on the closing plate, and the converter is over the front collector. The channels are beside the Pi window (12 at +y, 7 at −y), and with a blower fitted the 8 Pa the full-length fins cost is not the constraint, so they are finned end to end for the surface.
3. **The ring** (items 3, 5, 6; rules 7, 8, 10). The brief's openings through 12.5 mm of ring were not machinable; the plenum it called optional is what makes them so. The ring's underside has a groove — r 79.5–84 over the intake arc 60–300 (in two arcs, interrupted over the carriage hole), r 76.5–84 over the exhaust arcs 18–60 and 300–342, z 0–6.55, with 1.2° walls at 60 and 300, closed from below by the pad; a Ø9 land stands in it round each of the structure's three plate screws so their countersunk heads bear on metal — and the **120 positions on 3° (az 1.5 + 3k, mirrored about 0–180)** carry obround openings 2.0 × 4.5 (z 1.55–6.05) through the 3.7 outer wall wherever the groove lies behind: 64 intake + 28 exhaust. Not cut: the twelve across the port face (342–18); the eight over the motor's carriage hole (79–101 — the shoe's Ø38.5 hole reaches r 85 and cuts through the groove there, so openings would have looked into the cavity; the groove and the undercut are walled off either side of it); and the eight opposite (259–281), so the two side views are identical. No opening is blind: every cut one has the groove behind it, and the checker probes 2.5 mm behind the wall for it. Each mouth and both of the ring's edges carry a 0.3 × 45° chamfer (modelled as a taper; the drawing says electropolish after machining, then brush the edge face axially). No reeding: the three grooves the brief offered left 0.15 mm lands between them and clipped the openings' chamfered tops (Ryan's review, 7 Sep) — the upper land is plain. The 0.8 undercut under the outer wall opens straight into the groove all round both arcs, so it is the supplementary intake **and** the downward exhaust — the pad is a plain ring again, no voids. The passages that join the groove to the duct are the v14 grid at 11.25° with the ring screws on its lands, 9 wide: 17 intake (535 mm²) as grooves across the ring's inner land and **closed tunnels z 1–4.5 through the core's shoulder** (v14 cut them open to the underside, which joined the intake to the space under the plate), and three exhaust passages 10 wide at 311/320/329 for the −y side. Stainless (304/316), bare — `GROUNDING.md` 4.1 is closed: no masking, the star washer cuts through.
4. **The blower** (Ryan: required). No blower with a usable curve is 5 mm thick — the thinnest are Sunon's 30 × 30 × 3 at 0.3 L/s free air and 36 dB(A). The Delta BFB0305HA-C (datasheet in `vendor/`: 30 × 30 × 10, 5 V, 0.13 A, 7,500 rpm, 0.68 L/s free, 71 Pa at no flow, 29 dB(A), ball bearings, 10 g, two Ø2.4 holes on a 24 × 24 diagonal, AWG 30 leads 145 long) stands on the plate. A scan of the plate top found no free 30 × 30 spot with duct beneath it, so it took the motion board's, over the +y plenum at az 51, r 51, and **the motion board rides on the saddle** (z 20.0–23.6, 0.4 under the seat flange), its two diagonal screws being the blower's own through-bolts: M2 × 18 through the board, its 0.5 washers, the 1.2 saddle plate, the blower and the 0.3 inlet gasket into piers. Inlet face down over a Ø22 hole through the web, so it breathes only duct air. Its side outlet (21 × 7) opens into the saddle's hood, which turns the air down a **trench cut from the top** — 18 wide from r 66.8 out through the duct wall and the core's shoulder (126 mm²), widening to az 37–56 through a notch in the ring's flange and its inner land into the exhaust groove (whose roof is open there) — sealed by a separate flat lid on two M2 into the ring's flange, both on 0.3 foam gaskets. A 2 mm wall keeps the trench off the suction side, and the duct is solid outside r 64.8 between az 40 and 61 so no channel end or the collector meets it; the channels that used to end there stop at r 64.8 in the plenum (extended to az 22–66). Expected operating point about 0.5 L/s at ~25 Pa (fins 8, intake 3, trench and outlet 7, groove and openings 5): 0.6 W/K of air on top of the passive 0.5 — about 38 °C skin at 13 W in a 25 °C room. The −y side's 300–342 openings are pulled in reverse by the blower, so its seven channels see forced flow too; the discharge is the 18–60 arc only. Leads to the Pi 5's fan connector (JST SH 4-way, 5 V and GND — the connector is in the Pi's STEP, between its corner standoff and the USB-A stack), routed down the blower's corner, along its side face and over the Pi window's edge. PWM speed control from the Pi; 0.65 W; 29 dB(A) free-field — the acoustic question the plan raised stands and is a bench item.
5. **The structure inverted** (Ryan). Nothing rises above the seat flange's top face any more — it is the bed. The three nubs are Ø2 H7 through-holes (reamed) in Ø5 bosses hanging 4 under the flange, taking **ISO 8734 Ø2 m6 × 8 dowels** pressed from below (5.8 of grip, 2.2 proud, top 0.3 under the glass's top face); the pin at 60° moved to 70°, out from under the saddle. The encoder's two M2 inserts sit in bosses under the flange, the flange's holes are radial slots ±0.5 (so the sensor can be centred over the code ring), and **the 0.9 platform is a separate printed shim** — printed as a family 0.7/0.8/0.9/1.0/1.1 so the 2.0 optical gap is set on the bench. The bleed leaf's foot lies flat on the flange, its insert in a boss below; its preload is its own bend, and the same shims serve if the bench wants more. The pillar tops carry 45° cones and 45° webs up the wall, the LRA clip ribs 45° tops, the bed face's two circular edges 0.3 chamfers against elephant's foot. Supports: the diffuser lip (a 3.9 ring at 11.7 mm, outside the wall — the face it marks is under the knob skirt) and the three Ø8 wheel-post tops (the bush seats, at 7.1 mm). Bridges, no support: the three wheel windows' far ends (a 15 mm bridge in the 2 mm wall at 7.4 mm — they were open-topped upright, now they are roofs) and the flex recess's 4.2 mm span at 0.6 mm. v14 needed the whole seat flange supported.
6. **Review fixes (Ryan, 7 Sep)**: no reeding (0.15 mm lands, and the lowest groove clipped the openings' tops); 92 openings not 108 — eight over the motor's carriage hole and eight mirrored are not cut, and the groove is walled off from the hole; Ø9 lands in the groove round the plate screws; the fan lead on the Pi's real fan connector (in the STEP, between the corner standoff and the USB-A stack) after a checker rule that allowed anything touching a `pi_` body was fixed — which also exposed the 212° rib running through the Pi's corner standoff (it now starts at r 63.5); **the halo strip's tail**, which had no way out of the wall: the strip's joint at az 64 with a 4 × 3 notch through the wall's foot, and a three-wire tail modelled to a Pico-Lock on the motion board 60 mm away — so the halo's 5 V goes through the motion board (question 32); **the audio board's USB-A plug** moved from the Ethernet jack, where the model had it, into the lower socket of the USB 3.0 stack, with the bracket's −y ear at (46, −20), its foot notched, the plate's notch moved and the closing screw (44, −6) moved to (44, −22).
7. **Airflow (Ryan's review)**: a turning vane in the hood's neck, R2.5 on every vertical corner of the trench, and a clear Ø26 pocket in the duct under the blower's inlet — together roughly 15 Pa of the ~25 Pa system loss at 0.5 L/s, worth about 0.1 L/s on the blower's curve.
8. **Bonds**: the ring's bond screw moves to az 67.5 (56.25 lay in the trench's foot) — on the grid land, 3.4 of metal to the passage wall, 44 mm from the chassis bond. The chassis bond stays at (48, 27).

Carried from v14: the design-changes list's items (connectors, bare internal faces, grounding, halo current, pogo board, sensor loop); from v13: the encoder reading the crown, the seat at 26.0; from v12: the deck-less layout; from v11: the Ø175.4 knob and the heatsink plate.
