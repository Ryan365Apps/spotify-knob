## What changed against the specification while building, and why

1. **The LED ring lies flat on the plate, not on the outside of the wall.** A side-view LED on a flex wrapped round a cylinder fires up or down, not outward. Laid flat at r 58.8 it fires outward into the diffuser. Consequence: the diffuser moved out to r 60–62.5 (2.0–2.5 thick) and the ring is continuous under the motor because the motor's *base* is assumed narrower than its bell (see bench item 1).
2. **The speaker stands cone-up on its rear boss**, breathing into the cavity and out through the perimeter ports. "Down-firing" into a solid plate made no sense once the sound path moved to the ports.
3. **The code band is 4.2 mm tall** (18.4–22.6): the AEDR-8300 package is 3.96 across the strip. The wheels moved up to 23.4–27.4, which the board-edge rule still allows at 30/150/270.
4. **The port module is a printed face plus two small boards**: USB-C on a horizontal board at z 2.2 in the slot; the jack hanging under a board at z 5.0 so its body sits at z −1..5, below the halo; the light sensor on a tiny vertical board looking rearward through a Ø4.8 hole in the face. One board could not put the jack's axis at z 2 and the USB-C at z 2 at the same time.
5. **The display columns are 4 × 6 blocks at 42/138/222/318°, three degrees off the M4 holes**, with 1.2 mm tabs reaching the holes in the 1.5 mm gap above the board. Two of the four holes are 0.5 mm from the board's edge, so no post can stand under them.

## Bench items that decide whether v9 survives — measure on the real parts

1. **Motor base diameter ≤ 31 and bell starting ≥ 4.3 mm above the base face.** If the base is Ø35 the LED ring is interrupted ±11° at the motor; if the bell starts lower it fouls the lip. Nothing published; calipers.
2. **Which end of the shaft carries the magnet.** v9 puts the commutation board under the motor inside the carriage (sight hole Ø9). If JD-Power fits it at the top, the board moves and the display rises 3 mm (over the limit).
3. **Motor bolt pattern** — the carriage has only a Ø36 locating rim.
4. **Servo lugs and pushrod** — the mount is a tray; the pushrod-to-tab joint is not drawn.
5. **Band grip at 2.4 N** — if it slips, add the 2 : 1 bell-crank.
