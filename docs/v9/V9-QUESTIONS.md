# the 60 — version 9 open questions

**No open questions.** All twenty-eight were answered on 4 September 2026 and folded into `V9-SPECIFICATION.md`; the seven raised by those answers were closed by approving the defaults the same evening.

Settled by the final round, for the record in one place:

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | Motor is the JD-Power **MY-3514C** (2804 frame, Ø35 × 14 envelope) |
| 2 | Stack **case B**: a 4.2 mm carriage on the pad through a hole in the plate, commutation board inside the carriage under the shaft, motor bolted on top at z 4.2; overall 39.6 mm |
| 3 | **Plate 5 mm** thick (~340 g); halo bottom edge at z 5.0; sockets below it on a vertical port board; plug body in a notch through plate and pad |
| 4 | Servo pushes the carriage **directly, 2.4 N**; a 2 : 1 lever is the fallback if the band slips |
| 5 | Board's USB-C edge at the back; **wheels at 30°, 150°, 270°** |
| 6 | Microphones: **no ducts** on the prototype; the cavity is open through the perimeter ports; test dictation on the print |
| 7 | Perimeter ports: **24 × Ø2**, evenly spaced over the free arcs |
